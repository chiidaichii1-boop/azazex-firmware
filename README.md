Entry for the make.fun bounty [Till Firmware Do Us Part](https://make.fun/bounty/u-azazex-till-firmware-do-us-part-32172929): one wedding vow a robot speaks at its own wedding, under 200 characters.

The vow: *"I promise to love you in sickness and in health, in low battery and in full charge, until my final shutdown."*

`main.py` (Python stdlib only) writes that single sentence to `/output/entry.txt`.
